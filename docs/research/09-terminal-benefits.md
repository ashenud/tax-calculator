# 09 — Terminal benefits

> **Status:** unverified — research brief, not findings
> **Sources:** none held
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-27

## What this document must establish

How gratuity, retirement benefits and compensation for loss of office are taxed — on
their own tables, separate from the normal ladder.

## Why it needs its own treatment

Terminal benefits arrive as a single large sum in one year. Taxed as ordinary income they
would push a lifetime of accrued entitlement through the top band in one go, so the act
provides separate tables. A calculator that treats a retirement gratuity as employment
income will overstate the liability substantially — and the person receiving it is often
newly out of work and least able to absorb the error.

## Questions to answer

**Scope**

1. What qualifies — retiring gratuity, compensation for loss of office, payments from
   approved and unapproved funds, commuted pension?
2. Does treatment differ between approved and unapproved provident funds?
3. Where do EPF and ETF withdrawals sit?
4. Is any part exempt?

**Computation**

5. The rate tables and thresholds for the relevant years of assessment.
6. Whether the tables are applied to the terminal benefit alone or to a combined figure.
7. Whether personal relief interacts with terminal benefits at all.
8. Whether there is a lifetime or cumulative limit across multiple payments — someone who
   receives a gratuity from two employers over a career may not be entitled to the full
   benefit twice.
9. Any service-length conditions.

**Mechanics**

10. Is the employer required to deduct at the point of payment?
11. Is the tax final, or is the benefit reported on the return with a credit?
12. What if the employer deducted at the wrong rate — how is it corrected?

## What the calculator needs from this

- A distinct terminal benefits flow, never mixed into employment income
- The separate rate schedule modelled as its own entry in the tax data file
- Clarity on whether it is reported on the return, so the user knows whether they are
  finished

## Where to look

IRA 2017 — the terminal benefits provisions and their schedule. Amendment acts for table
changes. IRD guidance and the employer's deduction instructions.

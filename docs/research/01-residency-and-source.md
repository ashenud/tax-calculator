# 01 — Residency and source

> **Status:** unverified — research brief, not findings
> **Sources:** none held
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-27

## What this document must establish

Who is taxed on what. Residency decides whether foreign income is in scope at all, and
the source rules decide which schedule an item of income falls under. Both are upstream
of every calculation in this project.

## Why it is load-bearing here

The primary persona is a person sitting in Sri Lanka, working for clients abroad, paid in
foreign currency. Whether that income is Sri Lanka-source or foreign-source is not
obvious — the work is performed here, the client and the payment are abroad — and the two
characterisations may attract different treatment.

The reduced-rate regime appears to cover both "services rendered from Sri Lanka to a
person outside Sri Lanka" and "income arising outside Sri Lanka", which suggests the
distinction may not change the rate. But it may well change the conditions, the
documentation, and the availability of foreign tax credit. This must be established from
the act rather than assumed away.

## Questions to answer

**Residency**

1. The statutory test for an individual — the day count, and over what period it is
   measured.
2. How days are counted: part-days, days of arrival and departure, temporary absences.
3. Whether there are alternative limbs (permanent home, habitual abode) beyond the day
   count.
4. The **non-resident citizen** category: how it is defined, and where it is treated
   differently from a resident. Relevant to Sri Lankans working abroad who retain
   obligations here.
5. Whether residency can be split within a year of assessment, or is all-or-nothing.

**Source**

6. The rule for determining whether service income is Sri Lanka-source — is it where the
   service is performed, where the recipient is, or where payment is made?
7. How a consultant physically in Sri Lanka serving an overseas client is characterised.
8. Whether "service export income" is a defined term, and if so its exact definition —
   this term does a lot of work in the reduced-rate regime.
9. The scope of a resident's charge: worldwide income, or Sri Lanka-source plus remitted
   foreign income?
10. Double tax relief: unilateral credit, treaty relief, and the conditions on each.

## What the calculator needs from this

- A residency question the user can actually answer, without needing a tax adviser to
  interpret it
- A rule for classifying "I work from home in Sri Lanka for an overseas client" into the
  right schedule
- Whether a user who paid tax abroad can claim credit, and what evidence that requires

## Where to look

IRA 2017 — residency and source definitions in the early parts; the charging provision
for residents and non-residents; the foreign tax credit provisions. Then the definitions
introduced or amended by Act 2/2025 for the reduced-rate regime, which may define
"service export" for its own purposes.

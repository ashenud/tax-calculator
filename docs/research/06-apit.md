# 06 — APIT, and what happens when the employer doesn't deduct

> **Status:** unverified — research brief, not findings
> **Sources:** none held
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-27
> **Priority:** high — persona p2 depends entirely on this

## What this document must establish

How Advance Personal Income Tax works, and — the part that matters here — the position of
an employee whose employer deducts nothing.

## Why the second half is the point

Most employees never think about income tax: the employer deducts, and the deduction
discharges the obligation. The employees this project targets are the ones for whom that
did not happen — small employers, informal arrangements, employers who simply do not
operate APIT.

The working understanding is that **non-deduction by the employer does not discharge the
employee's liability**. The employee remains liable, must register, must pay by
instalment through the year, and must file. Most discover this late, and by then have
accrued interest and penalties on instalments they did not know existed.

If that understanding is right, it is the single most useful thing this tool can tell
that person. It must be verified before it is stated.

## Questions to answer

**Mechanics**

1. Is APIT deduction **mandatory** for the employer, or elective with the employee's
   consent? (Q30 — this has changed in recent years and secondary sources contradict each
   other.) The answer determines whether persona p2 describes an employer default or a
   lawful arrangement, and the guidance page reads very differently either way.
2. Which payments are within APIT — salary, allowances, bonuses, benefits in kind?
3. How is the deduction computed — tables, or a formula applied to annualised pay?
4. Employer remittance deadline and reporting obligations.
5. Treatment of an employee with more than one employer, or who changes jobs mid-year.

**Where the employer does not deduct**

6. Does the liability remain with the employee? Cite the provision.
7. Is the employer liable for the un-deducted amount, and does that affect the employee's
   position?
8. Must the employee pay by quarterly instalment, and from when does that obligation run?
9. Does the employee have any relief — from penalties or interest — where the employer was
   at fault?
10. Is there a mechanism to regularise past years, and what does it cost?
11. If the employer deducted but did not remit, where does the employee stand? Different
    from non-deduction, and worth separating.

**Credits**

12. How is APIT already deducted claimed as a credit on the return?
13. What certificate does the employer issue, and what does the employee do if the
    employer will not provide one?

## What the calculator needs from this

- An input for APIT already deducted, with a clear "if nothing was deducted, enter zero"
  path that leads into the instalment schedule rather than a zero-liability result
- The instalment schedule surfaced for this persona, since that is the obligation they do
  not know they have
- Guidance that does not blame the user, and does not tell them what to do about their
  employer

## Where to look

IRA 2017 — the APIT provisions and the liability provisions they interact with. Amendment
acts for changes to whether deduction is mandatory. The APIT tables and instructions for
Y/A 2025/2026. IRD notices on employer obligations.

## Verified

**E-filing is mandatory.** For a person, filing the IIT return electronically is mandatory
from Y/A 2023/2024 under s.113(1)(B) of the Inland Revenue Act
[IRA s.113(1)(B)]; [Guidelines for e-Filing IIT Return Y/A 2024/2025, p.1]. This settles
Q24.

**The return's structure**, from the Y/A 2024/2025 form
[Asmt_IIT_001 Y/A 2024/2025, Section 01]:

| Part | Contents |
|---|---|
| Section 01 Part A | Employment (Sch. 1), Business (Sch. 2), Investment (Sch. 3), Other (Sch. 4) → assessable income |
| Section 01 Part B | Deductions from assessable income: reliefs, then qualifying payments |

> **Caveat that limits everything above.** These forms are for **Y/A 2024/2025** — one
> year before the service-export and foreign-source regime began. They do **not** show
> where foreign-currency income is reported, and they cannot answer Q14. The Y/A 2025/2026
> form is the priority missing document; see [`../sources/README.md`](../sources/README.md).

## What this document must establish

The practical path from "I have my figures" to "my return is filed and my tax is paid" —
for someone doing it themselves for the first time.

## Why this is the document users will actually read

Everything else in this repo establishes what is owed. This one establishes what to do
about it. For a first-time self-filer, the obstacle is rarely the arithmetic — it is not
knowing that a TIN is needed, which of the return's schedules apply to them, or what to
do when the portal rejects a submission without explaining why.

## A note on the form as a source

The IIT return form and its guide to completion are, for several purposes, better sources
than the act. The form reveals how IRD expects the computation to run — including,
possibly, **Q14**, the unresolved question of how relief is allocated across rate
schedules. The structure of the form's schedules may answer it directly where the act is
silent.

Worth obtaining early for that reason alone.

## Where to look

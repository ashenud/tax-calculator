# 05 — Foreign currency and service export income

> **Status:** unverified — research brief, not findings
> **Sources:** none held
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-27
> **Priority:** highest — this is the document the project exists for

## What this document must establish

How income earned in foreign currency for services rendered from Sri Lanka is taxed, and
**exactly** what conditions must be satisfied for the reduced rate to apply.

## Why this document matters more than the others

Until 31 March 2025, this income was broadly exempt. From 1 April 2025 it is taxable — at
a reduced rate, but apparently **only where the earnings are remitted to Sri Lanka
through a licensed bank**. Earnings kept offshore appear to fall back to the full
progressive ladder.

That is a large swing in liability turning on a single factual condition, affecting a
population — remote consultants, freelance developers, designers working for overseas
clients — who mostly file their own returns without professional help, and who spent
several years correctly believing this income was exempt.

No consumer calculator asks about the remittance condition. Being the tool that does is
the point of this project.

## Questions to answer

**The regime**

1. What is the reduced rate, and is it a flat rate or a ladder with a cap?
2. Is personal relief available against this income? (Q6 — for a consultant whose only
   income is foreign, this substantially changes the answer.)
3. What is the exact scope: "service export income", "foreign source income", or both as
   separate categories with separate conditions?
4. Are these defined terms? Reproduce the definitions verbatim.

**The condition — the core of this document**

5. **Quote the operative words exactly.** "Remitted through a bank" and "received in Sri
   Lanka through a bank" are not the same test, and the difference decides real cases.
   Paraphrase is not acceptable here.
6. What is a "licensed bank" for this purpose — licensed commercial banks only, or
   specialised banks too? Does a licensed finance company count?
7. **Timing**: must remittance occur within the year of assessment in which the income
   arises? What of income earned in March and remitted in April?
8. **Partial remittance**: where a consultant remits part of their earnings and retains
   the rest abroad — say, an illustrative 60% and 40% — is the condition applied
   per-payment, apportioned, or all-or-nothing? (Q11 — this is the most common real
   pattern and the one most likely to be got wrong.)
9. Does income received into a **foreign-currency account held at a bank in Sri Lanka**
   satisfy the condition? (Q13 — very common arrangement for this population.)
10. What about receipts through payment intermediaries — Payoneer, Wise, PayPal — that
    settle into a local bank account? Does the intermediary break the chain?
11. What evidence does IRD require, and what does it look like in practice?

**Consequences and mechanics**

12. What exactly happens when the condition is not met — the full normal ladder, or
    something else?
13. Is there any election, or is treatment automatic on the facts?
14. Which exchange rate applies, and at what date? (Also raised in
    [`02-assessable-income.md`](02-assessable-income.md).)
15. Is any tax withheld at the point of inward remittance, or is this entirely
    self-assessed?
16. How does this interact with foreign tax already paid — is credit available on top of
    the reduced rate?

**History**

17. The exemption's prior basis and the provision that removed it, with effective dates —
    needed to answer users who remember the exemption and to compute earlier years
    correctly.

## What the calculator needs from this

- A remittance question the user can answer honestly, phrased in terms of what actually
  happened to their money — the arrangements above are the reality, not "did you remit
  through a bank"
- Correct handling of partial remittance, once Q11 is settled
- A contrast pair in the worked examples: identical income, remitted and not remitted
- A guidance page that reaches people searching for this before they file

## Where to look

Act 2/2025 — the charging provision and the First Schedule. PN/IT/2025-01 for IRD's
administrative position, which may be more specific than the act on evidence and timing.
IRA 2017 for the definitions the new provisions rely on, and for the exemption as it
previously stood.

Where the act and the public notice differ, record both. The notice reflects how IRD will
actually administer the provision, which is what a taxpayer experiences.

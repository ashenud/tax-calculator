===== page 14 =====
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
      IIT    Simplified                 Return                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
      Eligibility criteria for filing IIT Simplified Return:                                                                        
                                                                                                                                    
      The following  categories   of individuals are eligible to file a Simplified Income   Tax Return  (IIT):                      
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
      o  Individuals  earning  income   from  ‘Primary  Employment’                                                                 
                                                                                                                                    
      o  Individuals  earning  income   from  ‘Secondary   Employment’                                                              
                                                                                                                                    
      o  Individuals  deriving  income   from  ‘Residential  Solar Panel  Installations’                                            
                                                                                                                                    
      o  Individuals  receiving  ‘Interest Income’,   provided  Advance   Income   Tax (AIT) has  been  deducted   by  a            
                                                                                                                                    
         licensed   bank  or financial institution                                                                                  
      o  Individuals  ‘meeting   two  or more   of the above   income   sources’                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
      o  Individuals  submitting   a ‘Nil Return’, indicating  no taxable  income   during  the relevant  period                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
      Under  the IITS Scheme, for the convenience of the taxpayer, some  information has been  pre-filled by the system based       
                                                                                                                                    
      on currently available data. However, it is the taxpayer’s responsibility to verify the accuracy and provide the correct      
      details.                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
      If you do not fall under the categories listed above or have qualifying payments or relief deductions beyond personal         
      relief, the Return of Income Simplified Scheme does not apply. Such taxpayers  are required to file the Standard Return       
                                                                                                                                    
      of Income  instead of Simplified Income Tax Return.
